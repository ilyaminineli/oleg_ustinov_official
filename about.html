Oleg Ustinov · 1984

Oleg Ustinov is an artist working across painting, graphics, collage, sculptural objects, multimedia installations and sound.

Born in Rostov-on-Don. In 2011 he entered the Moscow School of Photography and Multimedia, and in 2014 completed one of the school's early diploma projects in painting.

Ustinov's practice explores non-figurative image-making at the intersection of painting and digital processes. His method moves between classical painting and digital printing, creating a hybrid space between analogue image, post-digital painting and object.

He lives and works between Rostov-on-Don and Moscow.
